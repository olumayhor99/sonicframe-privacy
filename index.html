SonicFrame Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026

The short version

SonicFrame does not collect, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code in the app. Everything you make stays on your device.

Your projects and media

Videos you import, voice recordings, music you add and the files you export are stored only in SonicFrame’s private storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else. They are included in your own device backup (iCloud or your computer), which you control. Deleting a project deletes its copy of your media.

Microphone

SonicFrame uses the microphone only while you are recording a voiceover. Recordings are saved to your device and nowhere else.

Speech recognition

Transcripts are created entirely on your device with Apple’s speech recognition, with on-device processing required. If your device can’t transcribe a language on device, SonicFrame doesn’t transcribe it — your audio is never sent to Apple, to us or to anyone else.

Photos

When you import, the system photo picker gives SonicFrame only the videos you choose; SonicFrame can’t browse your library. Saving an export to Photos uses add-only access.

Purchases

SonicFrame Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; SonicFrame only receives confirmation that the purchase is valid and never sees your payment details. Apple’s privacy policy covers the purchase itself.

Sharing, Shortcuts and widgets

Files leave SonicFrame only when you share or save them yourself, or when a Shortcut you run returns them. Widgets show no personal information. A diagnostics report, if you choose to create one, contains only technical details (timings, track layout, effect settings, device model) — never your media, names or text — and is shared only by you.

Children

SonicFrame collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on this page with a new effective date.
